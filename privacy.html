Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ball Journey ("the App", "we", "us") collects and how it is used. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal information on our own servers. The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data on their own behalf:

  • Google Play Games Services — used for sign-in and leaderboards. When you connect your Google account, your public Play Games profile (player ID, display name, avatar) and game scores are shared with Google to power leaderboards and achievements.
  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — used to process in-app purchases (such as removing ads). Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play and is never seen or stored by us.

Locally Stored Data

Game progress, settings, and in-game currency are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is served in accordance with Google's applicable policies for child-directed treatment where relevant.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties beyond the service providers listed above, which process data under their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
